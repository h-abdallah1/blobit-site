Common questions

How do I restore Remove Ads on a new device?
Open Blobit and tap RESTORE at the bottom of the main menu, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Does Remove Ads remove every ad?
It turns off the ads between runs. Optional video ads (to continue a run or double your stars) stay available, and you only see them if you choose to.

How do I reset my progress?
Progress is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app erases it.
